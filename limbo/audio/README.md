| `8bit-pulso.mp3`, `8bit-a1-norte.mp3`, `8bit-quero-mais.mp3` | The other 8-bit versions, the bonus at the end (CHIPTUNES in Drive) |

**Adding them.** Rename the files as above. Open this folder on GitHub, press "Add file",
then "Upload files", drop them in, and press "Commit changes". The game finds them on its
own within a few minutes. A track that isn't here yet is read instead of heard.

**Size.** GitHub takes files of up to 25 MB from the browser. Export at 320 kbps or less:
a 5-minute song at 320 kbps is about 12 MB.

**Who can get them.** Anything here can be downloaded by anyone who finds it. If the label
has a say over the album's files, check with them before uploading.
